HOW IT WORKS

Our Custom Order Process

Clear steps, fast turnaround, no surprises.

1 · Inquiry

Send your bag type, size, quantity and artwork (or a rough idea). We reply with questions and a quote within 24 hours.

2 · Solution & Quotation

We recommend the material structure and printing method, then send a detailed FOB/CIF quote and timeline.

3 · Artwork & Proof

Send your design (AI/PDF preferred). Our team lays out the dieline and sends a digital proof for approval.

4 · Sampling

We produce physical samples — digital or gravure — and ship them to you for seal, print and fit checks.

5 · Production

After sample approval and deposit, we run bulk production with in-line quality checks on print and sealing.

6 · Delivery

Balance payment against B/L copy, then we ship to your port or door with full documentation support.

MATERIAL GUIDE

Choosing the Right Material

The structure depends on what you're packing and how long it needs to stay fresh.

▤

Kraft Paper

Natural, eco-friendly look. Often laminated with foil or PE for barrier. Popular for coffee and organic food.

◼

Aluminum Foil

Maximum barrier against light, oxygen and moisture. Best for coffee, mylar and long-shelf-life products.

♻

Recyclable Mono-material

All-PE or all-PP structures that are fully recyclable. Meets sustainability goals without giving up barrier.

❀

Compostable

Plant-based films that break down under composting conditions. Ideal for eco-conscious brands.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

Typically 5,000 pieces per design for gravure printing. Digital printing can handle smaller test runs — ask us for details.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes. We can send stock samples free of charge (you cover shipping), or make custom samples after artwork approval. Sampling takes about 7–10 working days.

Do you help with design?

Yes. Send us your logo and rough layout and our design team will prepare the dieline and print layout at no extra cost.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are 30% deposit and 70% balance against the B/L copy. Other arrangements can be discussed for established partners.

How long is production?

Usually 15–20 working days after sample approval and deposit, depending on quantity and print complexity.

Can you keep our design confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA and produce under neutral packing so your supplier and artwork stay private.
