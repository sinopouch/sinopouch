FAQ

Common Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Digital printing from 1,000 pcs and gravure printing from 5,000 pcs, depending on the format and design.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. Stock samples for quality checks, or custom samples after artwork approval. Custom sampling takes about 7–10 working days.

How many colors can you print?

Up to 10 colors with gravure printing. We also offer digital printing for small runs and test orders.

What is the lead time?

Usually 15–25 days after artwork approval. Samples take 3–10 days. Shipping is quoted separately.

What are your payment and trade terms?

Typically 30% deposit with balance before delivery. We work with EXW, FOB and CIF. Wire transfer is standard; L/C for large orders.

Are your bags food-safe?

We produce to FDA and BRC related standards and can provide material test reports on request. Note: the FDA does not "approve" packaging itself — it approves food-contact materials.
