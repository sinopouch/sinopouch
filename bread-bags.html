ABOUT BREAD BAGS

Bakery Packaging That Sells

Bread needs to breathe while staying fresh. Our bakery bags use anti-mist windows so customers can see the product clearly, with tin ties or zippers for easy resealing. Available in kraft or clear film, printed with your bakery branding to turn every loaf into a walking advert.

FAQ

Bread Bags FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity?

From 1,000 pcs with digital printing; 5,000 pcs with gravure printing.

Can I get samples first?

Yes — stock samples for quality checks, or custom samples after artwork approval in 7–10 working days.

How long is production?

Usually 15–25 days after artwork approval. Shipping is quoted separately.

Can you print my logo and design?

Yes, up to 10 colours with gravure, or digital printing for small runs. We help with the dieline and layout.

Are the materials food-safe?

We produce to FDA and BRC related standards and can provide material test reports on request.
