ABOUT STAND UP POUCHES

Stand Up Pouches, Made Your Way

The stand-up pouch (doypack) is the most versatile format in flexible packaging. A gusseted base lets it stand on shelf, while a resealable zip keeps contents fresh. Add a window, tear notch or spout, and choose from kraft, foil or recyclable mono-materials. Perfect for snacks, coffee, food, supplements and more.
